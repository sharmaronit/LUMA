# Luma privacy

Luma has no account, no analytics and no ads. It does not collect, sell or send your personal data anywhere.

## What stays on your TV
- Your apps, favourites, layout, wallpapers and settings are stored only on this TV. Backups are files you choose to save.
- Notifications: if you allow notification access, Luma reads your TV's notifications only to show them in its notification panel. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
- Usage access (optional) is used only to order recent apps and show Wi-Fi usage on this TV.
- Sleep timer: if you allow Luma to turn the TV off, it uses that permission only to switch the screen off when your timer ends.
- Clean my TV: if you allow USB debugging, Luma connects to this TV's own debugging service, on this TV only, to list apps and switch the ones you choose off or back on. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## When Luma uses the internet
- Programme artwork: posters shown in "From your apps" are downloaded from the addresses your streaming apps provide.
- Updates: when update checking is on, Luma asks GitHub for the latest Luma release. No personal data is included.
- Voice search: speech is handled by your TV's own Android speech service, under Google's or your TV maker's privacy terms. Luma only receives the recognised text.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings, or uninstall Luma to remove all of its data.
