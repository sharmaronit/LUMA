Depth wallpapers

A clock that lives inside the scene.

Every wallpaper is cut by hand so the time sits behind the peaks, dunes and treelines — placed where each photo has room to breathe.

  • 14 real photographs: mountains, beaches, dunes, lakes and aurora
  • Frosted glass digits, drawn once a minute so it never slows the TV
  • Browse full screen with ◀ ▶ and see exactly how Home will look
Quick settings

Everything important. Nothing extra.

Select the clock to open a compact glass panel: network, Bluetooth, sound, a sleep timer, wallpapers, StandBy and who's watching.

  • Sleep timer that really turns the TV off
  • Profiles — each person gets their own favourites, wallpaper and font
  • Recent apps: press Home on Home, then ▲ to close an app
StandBy

When the remote rests, the TV becomes a clock.

After two quiet minutes Luma fades into a full-screen clock. Any button brings you straight back.

Built for the remote

Small touches that add up.

Luma is designed around a D-pad, a slow TV processor and a living room — not a phone.

Clean my TV

Switches off ads, tracking and preloaded extras from the TV itself. Nothing is deleted, and one press undoes it.

Fast on cheap boxes

Tuned on a 2 GB Mi Box. Lite mode turns heavy effects down automatically on slower TVs.

Stays awake on Home

The screen won't switch off while Luma is showing — StandBy keeps it useful instead.

Notifications

A small bell appears only when there is something new. Nothing leaves your TV.

Profiles

Up to six people, each with their own favourites, wallpaper, theme and clock.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your layout and settings stay on the TV.

Install

Three ways in. Pick yours.

All three install Luma and can switch off the bloatware that slows your TV down.

Easiest

On the TV only

  1. Install Downloader from the Play Store.
  2. Open it and enter github.com/sharmaronit/LUMA/releases/latest/download/luma.apk
  3. Install, open Luma and choose it as your Home app.
  4. Optional: Settings › Device › Clean my TV.
Works on every TV

With a computer

  1. Turn on USB debugging on the TV.
  2. Download luma.apk and run adb install luma.apk
  3. Run the debloat script from the repo: scripts/debloat
Hands-off

With an AI assistant

  1. Turn on USB debugging on the TV.
  2. Open Claude Code, Codex or a similar assistant.
  3. Paste PROMPT.md and answer its questions.
